01 // CURRICULUM ARCHITECTURE

Three core pillars, built from the ground up.

Every student starts from their actual comfort level — building rock-solid fundamentals without rote pressure.

Reading, grammar, handwriting, and spoken confidence — developed through interactive stories and daily practice.

Mathematics & Logic

Number sense, tables, arithmetic, and problem-solving patterns practiced until concepts become second nature.

Computer Basics

Touch typing, mouse & keyboard control, operating systems, and foundational digital literacy for the future.
